What is the Poetry Generator?

The Poetry Generator is a browser-based creative writing tool that helps you compose poems across a variety of styles — haiku, sonnet, free verse, limerick, acrostic, and more. Enter a theme, keyword, or emotional tone, select a poetry style, and the generator produces a structured poem using built-in language patterns and creative templates. All generation runs locally in your browser with no server calls required.

Whether you are a student working on a creative writing assignment, a casual writer exploring a new style, or someone looking for a heartfelt message, the Poetry Generator gives you an instant creative starting point that you can refine and personalise.

How to Use the Poetry Generator

Generating a poem involves these steps:

  1. Enter a theme, subject, or keyword in the Topic field (e.g., "autumn", "friendship", "the sea at dawn").
  2. Select a poetry style from the dropdown: Haiku, Sonnet, Free Verse, Limerick, Acrostic, or Rhyming Couplets.
  3. Choose a mood or tone if available: Romantic, Melancholic, Joyful, Reflective, or Humorous.
  4. Click "Generate Poem" to produce a poem that follows the structure and rhyme rules of the selected style.
  5. Read the output in the poem display area. Click "Generate Again" to produce a different variation on the same theme.
  6. Click "Copy Poem" to copy the text to your clipboard for use in a document, card, or social post.

Key Features

  • Multiple Poetry Styles: Supports Haiku (5-7-5 syllable structure), Sonnet (14-line iambic), Limerick (AABBA rhyme), Free Verse, and more.
  • Theme-Driven Output: Generates poems centred on your chosen topic rather than generic filler text.
  • Mood and Tone Control: Select the emotional register of the poem to match your intended message.
  • Instant Re-Generation: Produce multiple variations on the same theme with one click, giving you options to choose from.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use the generated poems commercially?
Yes. All poems generated by this tool are yours to use freely — for personal projects, greeting cards, social posts, or commercial content. No attribution is required.

Does the generator follow strict poetic rules?
For structured forms like Haiku and Limerick, the generator follows the standard syllable and rhyme rules. For Free Verse, output is thematic and expressive without strict constraints.

Can I input my own first line or rhyme?
The current version uses topic-driven generation. Future updates will include line-by-line assistance and custom opening verse support.

Are my poems saved anywhere?
No. Poems are generated and displayed locally in your browser. Nothing is saved to any server. Copy the poem to save it before closing the tab.

Privacy & Data Note

All poem generation runs entirely inside your browser's JavaScript engine. Your topic inputs and generated poems are never transmitted to any server, stored in any database, or used for any purpose beyond your current session.
