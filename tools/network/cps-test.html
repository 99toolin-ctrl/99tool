What is Infinite CPS Tester

The Infinite CPS Tester is a client-side click speed performance analyzer designed to measure your Clicks Per Second (CPS) over custom durations. By logging click events inside a responsive, trackable click box, the application calculates raw finger clicking velocity, rhythm, and input consistency in real-time.

Click speed is an essential metric in competitive eSports games, where actions are mapped directly to input rates. This tool offers a neutral testing ground to benchmark your hardware switch capabilities and test different clicking techniques under precise, millisecond-accurate timer sweeps.

How to Use Infinite CPS Tester

  1. Select your time duration: Click the duration mode buttons at the top to choose between **1 Sec**, **5 Sec**, or **10 Sec** intervals.
  2. Begin the test: Press your mouse button or tap your touch screen inside the dashed **Click here to start** box. The timer will launch on your first click.
  3. Click continuously: Click as fast as possible within the workspace. A red ripple effect will trace your cursor location inside the box.
  4. Check live outputs: Follow the dashboard boxes above the input area to read your remaining time, click count, and real-time CPS.
  5. Complete the benchmark: When the timer reaches zero, the workspace locks and displays the final click average.
  6. Reset: Click the **Reset Test** button to clear your score and run a new clicking speed benchmark.

Key Features

  • Multi-mode Duration Selectors: Quick configuration buttons to test speeds in short bursts or sustained clicks.
  • Interactive Ripple Feedback: Spawns absolute coordinate ripples inside the click zone to visually verify click registrations.
  • Responsive Touch and Mouse Handlers: Dual event listeners supporting standard gaming mice and mobile multi-touch gestures.
  • Precision Delta Calculations: Uses high-resolution browser performance timestamps to calculate speeds.

Common Use Cases

  • Competitive Action Practice: Refining jitter, butterfly, or drag clicking styles for PvP battles or clicker games.
  • Hardware Switch Audits: Comparing microswitch response speeds between different mice to identify the most responsive gaming gear.
  • Endurance Diagnostics: Analyzing how much your click speed drops as you move from a 1-second sprint to longer 10-second tests.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is a good Clicks Per Second (CPS) score?
A: A standard office worker typically clicks at about 3 to 6 CPS. Competitive gamers aiming for high actions-per-minute (APM) strive to hit 10 to 15 CPS using specialized finger techniques.

Q: How do different test durations impact CPS scores?
A: Shorter tests (like 1 second) measure your peak acceleration and raw muscle response. Longer tests (like 5 or 10 seconds) evaluate muscular endurance and rhythm consistency as fatigue sets in.

Q: Why does the click area display ripples under my cursor?
A: The ripples serve as visual confirmation that your clicks are registering in the browser thread. If you click and no ripple appears, your browser is lagging or dropping inputs.

Q: Can I run this clicking test on a tablet or phone?
A: Yes. The tester supports mobile layouts and features touch handlers that capture taps on mobile web browsers.

Privacy & Data Note

All click coordinates, intervals, and speeds are processed locally within your browser tab. Your inputs are never logged, stored, or sent to external servers.
