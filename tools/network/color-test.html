Why Test Your Display Colors?

Your monitor is the window to your digital world. But not all screens are created equal. Over time, or due to manufacturing variances, panels can drift from "true" colors. A display color test helps you identify if your screen has a "Yellow Tint," poor contrast, or "Color Banding" (where gradients look like steps instead of smooth transitions). Our online color checker provides high-fidelity patterns to audit your panel's health.

Testing for RGB Purity and Uniformity

By filling your screen with pure Red, Green, or Blue, our monitor color tool allows you to check for "Panel Uniformity." If the edges of your screen look darker or a different shade than the center, you are seeing backlight bleed or panel degradation. This online color quality test is a professional baseline for photographers and video editors who need a calibrated workspace.

Detecting Color Banding and Bit-Depth

If your smooth gradient test shows distinct lines or "blocks" of color, your monitor may be limited to a lower bit-depth (like 6-bit + FRC instead of 8-bit or 10-bit). Banding can make dark movie scenes look blocks and unprofessional. Use our display spectrum checker to ensure your hardware can transition between millions of colors without visible artifacts.
