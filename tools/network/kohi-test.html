What is the Infinite Kohi Click Test?

The Infinite Kohi Click Test is a classic, high-performance click diagnostics tool modeled after the standard clicking benchmark from the historical Kohi Minecraft server. Clicking speed, quantified in Clicks Per Second (CPS), serves as a core performance metric in competitive action games. The Kohi benchmark strictly utilizes a 10-second test window, balancing fast twitch finger speed with physical endurance.

This checker employs high-precision browser event handlers to capture click timestamps without processing latency. It computes your running CPS dynamically, providing a reliable diagnostic environment for competitive gamers and hardware evaluators.

How to Use the Kohi Click Tester

Follow these instructions to measure your clicking capability:

  1. Locate the large interactive "Kohi" target box in the center of the workspace.
  2. Click or tap inside the box to immediately trigger the automated 10-second timer.
  3. Click as fast as possible within the boundary until the timer runs down to zero.
  4. Monitor the dynamic digital readout to watch your live average CPS speed.
  5. Review your final clicks-per-second rating and total registered inputs.
  6. Click "Restart Test" to clear the score board and try again.

Key Features of the Kohi Auditor

  • Standard 10-Second Interval: Adheres to the classic timing threshold used by competitive PvP communities worldwide.
  • Zero-Latency Click Capture: Uses direct document listeners to ensure no clicking inputs are missed or delayed.
  • Responsive Target Box: Custom active styles trigger visual indicators for each click.
  • Mobile Compatibility: Adapts to touch gestures on smartphones and tablets, translating taps to CPS metrics.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the origin of the Kohi test?
The test originated on the popular Kohi Minecraft server as a custom diagnostic lobby widget designed to help players check their clicking speed before entering PvP arenas.

How many clicks per second is considered fast?
An average user clicks at 6 CPS. A score of 9 to 12 CPS is considered very fast, and elite players using specialized clicking methods can exceed 15 CPS.

What clicking techniques are used for the Kohi test?
Common clicking methods include standard clicking, jitter clicking (vibrating the arm), butterfly clicking (alternating two fingers), and drag clicking (using friction across the mouse button).

Will a double-clicking mouse raise my score?
Yes. Some gaming mice allow lowering the debounce time, which registers a single physical click as two inputs, raising your CPS score but potentially causing double-clicks in other applications.
