What is a Public IP Address?

In the digital world, an IP (Internet Protocol) address is your device's virtual mailing address. Every time you visit a website, participate in a video call, or download a file, your device uses its public IP address to communicate with the world. Our check my ip utility allows you to see exactly what information websites see when you connect to them.

IPv4 vs. IPv6

You might notice your IP looks like four numbers separated by dots (IPv4) or a long string of letters and numbers (IPv6). IPv4 is the classic standard, but because the world ran out of addresses, IPv6 was created to support billions of new devices. Our ip finder tool identifies whichever version your current network is using as its primary gateway.

Can People Find My Location via IP?

An IP address doesn't show your home address or phone number, but it does show your relative location—usually the city or town of your service provider's nearest hub. When you use our what is my ip tool, we show you the geo-location data associated with your connection. This is why websites sometimes show you local weather or localized currency automatically.

Is My IP Private at 99tool.in?

Absolutely. While we use external lookup APIs to display your details, 99tool.in does not store your IP address or create a database of user behaviors.
