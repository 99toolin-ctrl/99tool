What is the PowerPoint Viewer (Basic)?

The PowerPoint Viewer (Basic) is a client-side presentation previewer designed to extract and display text slides and embedded assets from PPTX files. By dropping a slideshow into the browser tab, you can read slides, review speaker notes, and check presentation outlines without launching a heavy application suite.

This viewer works entirely inside your browser's runtime memory, parsing the PPTX archive package locally. Because it does not upload your slides or graphics to external cloud platforms, your corporate pitch decks, sales projections, and proprietary charts remain completely private.

How to Use the PowerPoint Viewer (Basic)

  1. Upload Your PPTX File: Drag and drop your presentation file into the dotted upload area, or click inside the area to select the file from your computer.
  2. Select a Slide to View: The tool generates a slide index sidebar on the left. Click on any slide thumbnail card or slide number to display its contents.
  3. Review Text and Media: Read the extracted text blocks and inspect the embedded slide images displayed in the main content container.
  4. Copy Content Safely: Select and copy text directly from the slides to use in meeting summaries, lecture notes, or code logs.

Key Features

  • Lightweight XML Parsing: Extracts text data from PPTX containers instantly in browser memory.
  • Slide Sidebar Navigation: Browse through slide decks easily using a sequential thumbnail index.
  • Embedded Image Extraction: Decouples and renders photos, diagrams, and illustrations locally.
  • Zero Installation Required: Works entirely in your web browser without plugins or external libraries.
  • Complete Local Isolation: Keeps your business presentations safe by running 100% offline.

Common Use Cases

  • Pitch Deck Previews: Scan business layouts, executive summaries, and slides quickly before meetings.
  • Lecture Note Extractions: Extract slide text and outlines from school presentations to create study guides.
  • Design Asset Auditing: Inspect embedded slide images or diagrams to verify resolutions and visual styles.
  • Fast Document Checks: Verify the slide count and contents of an email attachment before importing it to editing suites.
