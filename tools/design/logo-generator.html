What is the Logo Generator?

The Logo Generator is a client-side branding utility designed to help you create clean, minimalist logos directly inside your browser. By combining custom typography, icon categories, and dual color selectors, you can design vector-like marks without using complex graphic editors.

This builder processes all canvas drawings and text alignments locally in your browser. Because no company names, taglines, or brand colors are sent over the network, your business concepts remain strictly confidential.

How to Use the Logo Generator

  1. Enter Brand Typography: Type your company name in the "Company Name" field and your tagline in the "Slogan / Tagline" field.
  2. Select a Layout: Choose a layout style (such as Icon Top, Icon Left, or Typography Only) from the layout dropdown menu.
  3. Pick an Icon: Select an icon category from the dropdown menu, then click on your preferred icon graphic in the selection grid.
  4. Customize Colors and Fonts: Set your primary, accent, and text colors using the color pickers, and choose a typography style from the font dropdown list.
  5. Download Your Logo: Review the live preview on the right canvas panel. Once satisfied, click "Download Logo" to save a high-resolution transparent PNG.

Key Features

  • Flexible Layout Options: Align icons above or alongside your text, or focus entirely on typographical marks.
  • Categorized Symbol Library: Choose from icons grouped by theme, including technology, business, education, and creative symbols.
  • Custom Palette Configuration: Set distinct primary, accent, and text colors to coordinate with your branding.
  • Modern Typography Styles: Customize your logo's appearance with a selection of popular clean web fonts.
