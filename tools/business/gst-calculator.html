What is the Infinite GST Tax Auditor (India)?

The Infinite GST Tax Auditor (India) is a direct client-side utility designed to calculate the Goods and Services Tax (GST) for commercial transactions under the Indian taxation framework. By inputting a transaction value and selecting the applicable tax slab, the tool computes base values, tax elements, and final totals. It serves as an instant auditor for invoicing compliance and retail transactions.

A key requirement in standard Indian GST invoicing is the separation of taxes into Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST) or Union Territory GST (UTGST). This tool automatically splits the total tax amount to help you generate accurate ledger entries. Because all calculations take place inside your browser tab, your transaction values and tax records remain fully confidential.

How to Use the Infinite GST Tax Auditor (India)

  1. Select Calculation Mode: Choose "GST Exclusive" if you want to add tax to a net base amount, or "GST Inclusive" if you want to extract the base tax elements from a total gross amount.
  2. Input the Amount: Type your transaction amount into the numeric field. This represents the Net Amount for exclusive calculations or the Gross Amount for inclusive calculations.
  3. Select the Tax Slab: Choose the target GST rate from the dropdown options: 5% (common household goods), 12% (processed foods/electronics), 18% (standard services and capital goods), or 28% (luxury items).
  4. Review the Tax Report: The results panel appears instantly upon typing. It details the Total Amount, Base Amount, Total GST, and the specific CGST and SGST splits.

Key Features

  • Bi-directional Calculations: Switch easily between adding tax (exclusive) and extracting tax (inclusive) with a single click.
  • Standard Indian Slabs: Built-in rate options covering 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28% to match standard GST council classifications.
  • Automated CGST & SGST Split: Automatically divides the calculated tax into 50% Central GST and 50% State GST for intra-state invoicing.
  • Instant Live Updates: View output shifts in real time as you adjust rates or type new numbers.
  • Secure Offline Processing: Client-side execution keeps sensitive business sales figures and ledger amounts safe from cloud tracking.

Common Use Cases

  • Freelance & Consulting Invoicing: Determine CGST and SGST splits to draft tax-compliant invoices for local clients.
  • Small Business Tax Auditing: Quickly cross-examine supplier bills and receipts to ensure that GST has been calculated correctly before entry.
  • Consumer Bill Audits: Verify whether a restaurant, hotel, or retail outlet is applying the correct GST rate to your final tab.
  • E-commerce Pricing Setup: Calculate backward from target consumer-facing inclusive prices to discover your actual base profit margins.
