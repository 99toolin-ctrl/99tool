What is the Infinite ROI & CAGR Auditor?

The Infinite ROI & CAGR Auditor is a financial auditing utility designed to measure the growth and annualized performance of your capital investments. It computes the absolute return on investment (ROI), net dollar profit, annualized rate of return (Compound Annual Growth Rate, or CAGR), and the total investment multiple. This gives you a clear picture of how effectively your money has worked over time.

A simple percentage gain does not account for the time required to achieve it. This utility solves that problem by integrating a time-adjusted CAGR metric, allowing you to compare short-term and long-term investment options on equal footing. Since all processing runs locally, your financial balances and asset metrics remain completely secure.

How to Use the Infinite ROI & CAGR Auditor

  1. Enter Initial Capital: Type the starting amount of your investment in the "Initial Investment ($)" input field.
  2. Input Final Asset Value: Type the current or ending value of your investment in the "Final Value ($)" input field.
  3. Define the Time Horizon: Enter the duration of the investment in years in the "Investment Length (Years)" field. You can input fractional years (e.g., 2.5) if needed.
  4. Analyze the Metrics: Review the results cards immediately. The tool shows your Absolute Return (ROI), net profit, Annualized ROI (CAGR), and the Investment Multiple (representing your cash-on-cash return).

Key Features

  • Absolute Return (ROI): Computes the raw percentage return from the initial capital basis to the final valuation.
  • Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR): Incorporates exponential growth math to determine your yearly rate of return.
  • Investment Multiple (Multiplier): Shows the exact cash multiplier (e.g., 2.5x your money) for the investment.
  • Dynamic Outputs: Results calculate automatically as you modify input numbers.
  • Confidential Sandbox Run: All calculations execute in local browser memory (RAM), ensuring your net worth data remains private.

Common Use Cases

  • Stock & Crypto Portfolio Tracking: Analyze the absolute and annualized returns of stock positions or token holdings over specific holding periods.
  • Real Estate Deal Comparison: Compare the performance of a property flip (held for 1.5 years) against a rental property purchase (held for 8 years).
  • Business Equipment Evaluations: Review the return on investment for purchasing new machinery or technology infrastructure by estimating cost savings over a set number of years.
  • Venture Capital & Angel Investments: Calculate investment multiples and annualized returns for early-stage startup funding deals.
