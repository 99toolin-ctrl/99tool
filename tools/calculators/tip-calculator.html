What is the Infinite Tip & Split Solver?

The Infinite Tip & Split Solver is an everyday financial utility designed to calculate gratuity and split bills among groups. It handles the math for dining out, shared transport, and group activities, dividing the final tab evenly across your party size.

The tool calculates the total tip, grand total (bill plus tip), and the exact amount each person owes. All computations happen in your browser sandbox, meaning your transaction amounts, gratuity rates, and party sizes remain fully private.

How to Use the Infinite Tip & Split Solver

  1. Enter the Bill Amount: Type the total pre-tip transaction amount into the "Total Bill Amount" input field.
  2. Select or Input Tip Percentage:
    • Click one of the preset buttons (10%, 15%, 20%, or 25%) to select a standard gratuity rate.
    • Or, type a custom percentage into the "Custom Tip %" field for alternative rates.
  3. Set the Party Size: Enter the number of people sharing the expense in the "Number of People" input field.
  4. Review the Cost Breakdown: Check the results panel, which updates as you type. It displays the Total Per Person, the Total Tip amount, and the Grand Total bill.

Key Features

  • Standard Tip Presets: Quick-select buttons for 10%, 15%, 20%, and 25% to speed up calculations.
  • Custom Gratuity Input: Support for custom rates to handle varying service quality standards.
  • Party Division Solver: Splits both the bill and the tip evenly across any number of people.
  • Dynamic Outputs: Results update in real time as you adjust amounts, tips, or group sizes.
  • Client-Side Processing: Keeps your personal spending and group dinner details confidential.

Common Use Cases

  • Group Dining and Restaurant Bills: Split dinner tabs and tips among friends or colleagues at the table.
  • Shared Transport and Taxi Fares: Calculate individual shares for taxi, shuttle, or rideshare costs including driver tips.
  • Holiday and Hotel Room Splits: Divide shared accommodation fees and service charges among travel companions.
  • Salon and Service Provider Tipping: Determine proper gratuity for personal care services like haircuts or spa treatments.
