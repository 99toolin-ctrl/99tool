What is the Infinite Ohm's Law Calculator?

The Infinite Ohm's Law Calculator is an interactive electrical circuit diagnostic designed to calculate the relationship between Voltage ($V$), Current ($I$), and Resistance ($R$). Ohm's Law is a core concept in electrical engineering and physics, defined by the formula $V = I \times R$. This equation states that the current flowing through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to the voltage across the two points, and inversely proportional to the resistance between them.

This utility allows you to enter any two known variables to immediately solve for the third missing value. It helps students, engineers, and makers visualize circuit parameters and verify theoretical designs.

How to Use the Ohm's Law Solver

You can calculate the missing value by inputting any two parameters:

  1. Identify the two values you already know (Voltage, Current, or Resistance).
  2. Enter the known variables into their respective input fields:
    • Voltage in Volts (V)
    • Current in Amperes (A)
    • Resistance in Ohms (Ω)
  3. Leave the field you want to solve for completely blank.
  4. Click the "Calculate" button to compute the missing variable.
  5. Review the solved value, which will automatically populate the empty input field, and inspect the formula breakdown in the result panel.
  6. Click "Clear" to wipe all fields and start a new circuit calculation.

Common Use Cases

This electrical calculator is designed for the following applications:

  • LED Resistor Sizing: Calculating the resistance required to limit current and protect LEDs from burning out.
  • Breadboard Circuit Audits: Checking current draws on prototype circuits to ensure power supplies are not overloaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Voltage?
Voltage is the electrical potential difference or force that pushes electric charge (electrons) through a conducting loop, measured in Volts (V).

What is Current?
Current is the rate at which electrical charge flows past a point in a circuit, measured in Amperes or Amps (A).

What is Resistance?
Resistance is the measure of opposition to current flow in an electrical circuit, measured in Ohms (Ω).

Can I enter milli-amps (mA) directly?
No. The calculator uses base SI units (Amps, Volts, and Ohms). You should convert milliamperes to Amps by dividing by 1,000 before entering the value.
