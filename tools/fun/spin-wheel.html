What is the Spin Wheel Random Picker?

The Spin Wheel Random Picker is an interactive, client-side decision-making utility developed to pick items or options randomly. By inputting custom lists and spinning the wheel, users can choose random names, raffle winners, or options using canvas physics animations.

This spinner performs all canvas animations, sector math, and rotational easing locally in your browser memory. Your list entries, options, and spin results are kept strictly inside your active browser tab, ensuring complete data security.

How to Use the Spin Wheel Random Picker

  1. Input Your Options: Enter your choices in the "Wheel Options" textarea, with one option per line. The canvas wheel redraws segments dynamically.
  2. Shuffle Choices: Click the "Shuffle Options" button to randomize the order of the segments on the wheel.
  3. Spin the Wheel: Click the "SPIN THE WHEEL" button to initiate the rotation animation.
  4. Read the Winner: Wait for the wheel to slow down and stop. Review the winner name highlighted in the alert overlay.

Key Features

  • Dynamic Sector Resizing: Automatically splits and adjusts the wheel sections based on your options count.
  • Realistic Easing Physics: Simulates a physical wheel spin with smooth deceleration math.
  • Shuffle Operations: Re-orders items instantly to make the spin outcome even more unpredictable.
  • Interactive Pointer: A clean indicator pointing to the winning segment upon stopping.
  • 100% Client-Side Processing: No server connections or database uploads are used.

Common Use Cases

  • Classroom Name Picker: Teachers picking students randomly for questions or presentation turns.
  • Lunch or Dinner Chooser: Settle group debate decisions on what type of food to order.
  • Raffles and Giveaways: Pick random winning tickets from an input list of participants.
  • Game Nights: Select challenges, topics, or players randomly for board games.
