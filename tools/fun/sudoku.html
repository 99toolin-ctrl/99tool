What is the Sudoku Generator & Solver?

The Sudoku Generator & Solver is a client-side puzzle utility designed to generate random Sudoku puzzles and solve them instantly. By adjusting difficulty settings, players can generate new board configurations, input numbers on a grid, and activate an automated backtracking solver to find solutions.

This Sudoku engine handles all grid allocations, uniqueness verifications, cell validations, and solver backtrack sweeps locally in your browser memory. Your game steps, solution times, and inputs remain completely private on your local machine.

How to Use the Sudoku Generator & Solver

  1. Set the Difficulty: Select your preferred level (Easy, Medium, or Hard) from the difficulty dropdown menu.
  2. Start a New Puzzle: Click the "New Game" button to clear the board and generate a new random puzzle. The timer starts automatically.
  3. Select a Cell: Click on any empty square in the 9x9 grid to highlight it.
  4. Input a Number: Click on one of the number pad buttons (1 to 9) in the sidebar, or press the corresponding number key on your keyboard. Click "Clear" to remove an entry.
  5. Use the Solver: If you get stuck, click the "Solve" button to let the backtracking engine calculate and fill in the complete correct grid.

Key Features

  • Dynamic Puzzle Generator: Generates unique board configurations across Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels.
  • Backtracking Solver Engine: Instantly calculates the complete correct solution for any valid puzzle layout.
  • Interactive Numeric Keypad: Sidebar buttons allow you to input numbers easily on touchscreens.
  • Live Session Timer: Tracks your game time dynamically to measure your solving speed.
  • 100% Client-Side Code: Runs entirely in your browser window, making it completely accessible offline.

Common Use Cases

  • Resolving Stuck Grids: Paste a puzzle from a newspaper or magazine into the grid and click "Solve" to find the solution.
