How Our Extraction Engine Works

PDF files don't actually contain "tables"—they contain characters placed at specific X and Y coordinates on a page. Our pdf extraction tool performs a complex spatial analysis:

  • Row Detection: We group text items by their vertical (Y) baseline, allowing for slight variances in alignment.
  • Column Clustering: We analyze horizontal (X) gutters to identify where one cell ends and the next begins.
  • Data Clean-up: Our logic removes redundant white space and ensures that currency symbols and percentages are preserved correctly.
  • Excel Generation: Finally, we use SheetJS to pack this structured array into a standards-compliant .xlsx file for instant download.
