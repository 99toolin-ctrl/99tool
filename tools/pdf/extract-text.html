The Logic of Modern PDF Parsing

PDF files aren't stored like Word documents. They are a collection of precise instructions on where to "draw" a letter on a page. This is why copying and pasting directly from a PDF reader often results in weird line breaks, missing spaces, or scrambled characters. A professional pdf text converter needs to reconstruct the logical flow of sentences by analyzing the coordinates of every character.

Our extract text pdf online free utility works by scanning every page and intelligently reassembling the text streams into a coherent format.
