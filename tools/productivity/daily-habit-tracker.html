What is the Daily Habit Tracker?

The Daily Habit Tracker is a private routine planner designed to help you build and maintain positive habits. Developing new habits requires consistent effort and clear visualization of your progress. Many online trackers sync your routine patterns to remote databases. By contrast, this tool runs entirely locally within your browser sandbox, keeping your personal routines secure.

By logging your daily progress, this tracker computes active streak durations. It uses your browser's local memory to save your habits, helping you track your progress without the need for an external account.

How to Use the Daily Habit Tracker

Starting and tracking your daily habits takes a few simple steps:

  1. Enter the name of your new routine in the text field at the top of the tracker dashboard (e.g., "Drink 3L Water" or "Read 15 Pages").
  2. Click the "Start" button or press Enter on your keyboard to create the habit card.
  3. Once you complete your habit for the day, click the circular checkmark button on the right side of the card. The button will show a checkmark and lock to indicate completion.
  4. Observe the "🔥 day streak" badge and the total completion counter update immediately.
  5. Click the trash bin icon (🗑) to delete a habit card and its progress data from your local dashboard.

Key Features of the Habit Solver

  • Automatic Streak Calculations: Tracks daily consecutive check-ins and resets your streak if a day is missed.
  • Locking Check-In System: Disables the completion toggle once a habit is marked done for the day to prevent accidental updates.
  • Cumulative Progress Counts: Logs total completions alongside active streaks, giving you a complete overview of your long-term success.
  • Clean Empty States: Displays helpful setup prompts when no active habits are registered in the tracker database.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is my streak calculated?
The tool compares the current date with the timestamp of your last completion. If you check off your habit on consecutive calendar days, your streak grows. If more than 24 hours pass without a check-in, the streak resets to 0.

Can I access my habits on another device?
No. To maintain complete privacy, all habit data is saved directly in your current browser's local storage. Your data does not sync across different browsers or devices.

Will clearing my browser cache delete my streaks?
Yes. Clearing your browser's site cookies and site data will delete your saved habits and reset all streaks.

Can I mark a habit done for a previous day?
No. The tracker is designed to log habits on the active calendar date to encourage real-time consistency and prevent retrospective logging.

Privacy & Data Note

All habits, timestamps, and streak statistics are processed locally in your browser's workspace. Your daily goals and accomplishments are never uploaded or tracked on remote servers.
