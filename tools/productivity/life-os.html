What is Life OS?

Life OS is a local personal organizer and dashboard utility designed to consolidate your tasks, habits, and knowledge notes into a single interface. Managing productivity often involves dispersing your data across multiple applications and third-party servers. This tool provides an offline cockpit that keeps your daily schedules and notes secure.

By operating locally, Life OS integrates statistics from your habits, tasks, and notes on one screen. Your entries are saved securely in your browser's private local storage, eliminating the risk of data leaks or server downtime.

How to Use Life OS

Managing your unified dashboard involves the following steps:

  1. Use the side menu buttons (Dashboard, Habits, Knowledge) to toggle between different modules.
  2. Type your primary target for the day inside the "Daily Focus" text box on the Dashboard.
  3. Click the quick-access links under "Quick Access Tools" to open specialized utilities like the Habit Tracker or File Locker.
  4. Use the "Knowledge" tab to type, review, and organize long-term notes in your local scratchpad.
  5. Click the "Export OS Data" button in the sidebar to download a backup file of your productivity database.

Key Features of the Command Center

  • Integrated Stats Panel: Displays real-time counts of your days streak, open tasks, and stored notes.
  • Context Switch Tabs: Organizes your workflow into clean frames for daily focus, habit logs, and note entries.
  • Offline Data Backup: Features an export engine to download your full database configuration as a JSON file.
  • Live Header Stats: Features a system-synced clock and dynamic greeting based on the hour of the day.

Common Use Cases

This personal OS is helpful for the following tasks:

  • Private Daily Journaling: Keeping sensitive personal reflections, business ideas, and daily achievements safe in a local sandbox.
  • Central Habit Monitoring: Observing streak counts and checklist progression across your local tools.
  • Workflow Backup Control: Exporting daily backup records to maintain full ownership of your data files.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my Life OS database stored?
Your notes, tasks, and habit data are stored locally in your browser's private local storage space. No data is sent to external servers.

Will clearing my browser cache delete my data?
Yes. Clearing your browser's site cookies and site data will clear your local storage. We recommend using the "Export OS Data" button to save backups regularly.

How do I import my data on a new device?
The export engine downloads your data as a JSON file. A dedicated import tool will be added in a future update to help you restore your files instantly.

Is there a limit to the length of notes I can store?
The note container is limited only by your browser's local storage capacity. You can log thousands of words without performance lag.

Privacy & Data Note

All focus items, dashboard statistics, personal notes, and tab configurations are processed locally on your computer. Your organizer data is stored securely in your browser's local storage and is never uploaded to remote servers.
