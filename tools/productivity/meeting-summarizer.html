What is the Meeting Notes Summarizer?

The Meeting Notes Summarizer is a rule-based note extraction tool that scans your pasted meeting notes and automatically categorizes lines into Action Items, Decisions, and Important Points. Standard cloud-based summarizers send your confidential meeting content to external AI engines. This tool performs all parsing locally inside your browser tab.

By using keyword prefix detection, it identifies structured intent markers in your text and extracts them into a clean Live Summary panel on the right. Your raw notes and their summaries never leave your device.

How to Use the Meeting Notes Summarizer

Extracting the key points from your notes involves these steps:

  1. Paste your raw meeting notes into the editor pane on the left side of the interface.
  2. Prefix action items with "Action:", "Task:", or "TODO:" to mark them for extraction.
  3. Prefix decisions with "Decision:", "Agreed:", or "Outcome:" to flag them as resolved points.
  4. Prefix highlights with "Important:", "Key:", or "Goal:" to mark them as critical notes.
  5. Watch the Live Summary panel on the right automatically populate as you type.
  6. Click "Copy Clean Summary" to copy the extracted highlights to your clipboard.

Key Features

  • Pattern-Match Engine: Detects keyword prefixes and bullet-point intent markers to extract structured content.
  • Three-Category Output: Organizes extracted content into Action Items, Decisions, and Important Points sections.
  • Live Summary Panel: Updates the extraction results in real time as you type or paste notes.
  • One-Click Copy: Copies the formatted summary to your clipboard for immediate sharing via email or chat.

Common Use Cases

This summarizer is helpful for the following tasks:

  • Corporate Meetings: Quickly extracting follow-up actions and decisions from lengthy strategy sessions before distributing minutes.
  • Project Standups: Isolating blockers, assignments, and resolved items from daily team updates.
  • Legal Briefings: Identifying agreed-upon terms and key outcomes from sensitive client meetings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool use AI to understand my notes?
No. This is a rule-based pattern matcher, not an AI. It finds lines you have prefixed with specific keywords (like "Action:" or "Decision:"). It does not interpret or paraphrase your content.

Is my meeting content safe?
Yes. All parsing happens inside your browser tab. Your notes never leave your device and are not stored anywhere.

Can I customize the trigger keywords?
The current version uses a built-in list covering standard meeting terminology (Action, Task, TODO, Decision, Goal, Important). Custom keywords may be added in a future update.

Does my raw notes text get saved?
No. The notes input field is not persisted to localStorage. Each session starts with a blank editor, so your sensitive content is automatically discarded when you close the tab.

Privacy & Data Note

All meeting notes, keyword extractions, and copied summaries are processed entirely inside your browser. No note content is stored, transmitted, or processed by any server or external AI service.
