What is the Browser Reminders Tool?

The Browser Reminders tool is a local scheduling utility that delivers system-native notifications directly to your desktop or mobile device. Standard digital planners often sync your schedule with external cloud databases, which poses a privacy risk. In contrast, this reminder app runs completely offline within your browser, ensuring that your tasks, appointments, and habits remain private.

By leveraging the browser's native notification system, this utility triggers timely alerts. It stores your tasks in your local browser sandbox, giving you a private schedule manager that survives browser restarts.

How to Use the Browser Reminders App

Creating and managing your local alerts involves the following steps:

  1. Click the "Allow Now" button on the permission banner to grant the browser authorization to display native notifications.
  2. Enter a brief, descriptive name in the "What's the reminder for?" text input box.
  3. Specify the exact alert date and time using the "When should we notify you?" datetime-local picker.
  4. Click the "Create Reminder" button. The new task will appear under "Active Reminders".
  5. Ensure the browser tab remains open or pinned in the background so the scheduling loop can trigger the alert.
  6. Click the "×" button next to any reminder to delete it, or click "Clear Expired" to clean up past entries.

Key Features

  • System-Native Alerts: Integrates with your operating system's notification center for consistent visual prompts.
  • Persistent Storage: Automatically saves your reminder list in local storage so you do not lose your agenda when refreshing the page.
  • Automatic Checking Loop: Runs a background check loop every 10 seconds to ensure notifications are delivered on time.
  • Queue Management: Allows you to clear expired items and manage multiple active alerts simultaneously.

Common Use Cases

This utility is helpful for the following tasks:

  • Desk Stretch Reminders: Setting regular reminders to stretch, hydrate, or rest your eyes during long work sessions.
  • Meeting Alerts: Scheduling meeting reminders so you do not miss video calls while working in other tabs.
  • Task Deadlines: Setting quick timers for tasks due later in the day to help you stay on track.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will I get notified if I close the tab?
Web browsers cannot send notifications if their corresponding tab is completely closed. We recommend keeping this tool open in a pinned tab in the background to ensure you receive alerts.

Does this tool require internet access?
No. Once the page is loaded, the reminder manager runs offline. It does not send your data to external servers.

Why didn't my notification make a sound?
Notification sounds are managed by your operating system's settings. Check if your device is in "Do Not Disturb," "Focus Assist," or silent mode.

Can I set repeating reminders?
This application is designed for one-time alerts. For recurring daily routines, we recommend using our Habit Tracker.

Privacy & Data Note

All reminder details, alert timings, and notification actions are processed locally on your device. Your data is stored securely in your browser's local storage and is never uploaded to external servers.
