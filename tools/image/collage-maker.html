What is Photo Collage Maker

The Photo Collage Maker is a local-first canvas compilation utility that allows you to arrange multiple images into customized grid layouts. By dropping your photos into the tool, you can select standard configurations, customize spacing/rounding, and download a unified composition in seconds.

This grid layout rendering is performed entirely client-side using the HTML5 Canvas API in your browser. Since no images are uploaded to any external server, your private memories, family photos, or proprietary project graphics remain completely safe and secure.

How to Use Photo Collage Maker

  1. Load your photos: Click inside the dashed upload zone to select multiple files from your device, or drag and drop your photos directly into the browser.
  2. Select your layout preset: Under the Layout section in the right sidebar, choose your desired grid style, such as Side-by-Side (🌓), Top-and-Bottom (⏳), Four Square (🪟), Triple Vertical (🚦), or Triple Horizontal (🛤️).
  3. Adjust the spacing: Drag the Spacing slider to set the thickness of the margins between each photo in the grid.
  4. Smooth the corners: Drag the Border Radius slider to round the edges of each cropped frame in the layout.
  5. Pick a background color: Click the Background Color picker to choose a color to fill the gaps between your photos.
  6. Manage your queue: Review your loaded photos listed under the Images section in the sidebar. Click the red × icon next to any filename to remove it from the collage.
  7. Download your collage: Click the Download Collage button to export the finished high-resolution PNG image directly to your local computer.

Key Features

  • Multiple Grid Layouts: Choose from five versatile preset arrangements to display up to 4 photos cleanly.
  • Smart Cover Fitting: Automatically crops and centers each photo to fill its designated cell without stretching or distortion.
  • Custom Spacing & Rounding: Adjust gaps and corner smoothness dynamically with visual sliders.
  • Dynamic Slot Management: Add or remove photos from your layout queue on the fly with instant canvas updates.
  • High-Resolution PNG Export: Renders the compiled collage at a high-fidelity 2000x2000 pixel output size.

Common Use Cases

  • Social Media Enthusiasts: Combining travel snapshots, holiday memories, or event highlights into cohesive grid posts.
  • E-commerce Sellers: Displaying different angles, details, or color options of a product in a single high-impact image.
  • Real Estate Agents: Presenting a multi-room preview or a before-and-after renovation layout.
  • Designers: Organizing quick mood boards or visual comparison sets for client presentations.

Privacy & Data Note

Every step of the grid rendering and image clipping is done locally on your computer. Your files are never stored or processed on external servers.
