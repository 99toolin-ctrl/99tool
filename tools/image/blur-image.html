What is Privacy Image Blur Tool

The Privacy Image Blur Tool is a selective censoring utility designed to hide sensitive information in documents or photos. Using browser Canvas filters, the tool allows you to paint a high-intensity Gaussian blur directly over specific areas of your image, or blur the entire file instantly.

This process runs entirely locally within your browser tab. Since no files are uploaded to any server, your personal details, financial figures, faces, or location signs are protected from third-party storage or analysis.

How to Use Privacy Image Blur Tool

  1. Upload your image: Click inside the dashed upload zone or drag and drop your image file (JPG, PNG, or WEBP) into the browser window.
  2. Choose blur mode: In the sidebar, select either Brush Blur to selectively paint over details, or click Total Blur to apply blur across the entire image.
  3. Configure brush and intensity: Drag the Brush Size slider to adjust the diameter of your brush, and drag the Blur Intensity slider to set the level of Gaussian blur (measured in pixels).
  4. Obscure the details: If using Brush Blur, click and drag your cursor directly over sensitive text, faces, or license plates on the canvas.
  5. Reset if needed: If you make a mistake or want to revert all changes, click the Reset All button to reload the original image.
  6. Download your censored image: Click the Download Censored Image button to export and save the finalized secure image to your local storage.

Key Features

  • Selective Painting Brush: Allows you to censor specific zones of a photo while keeping the rest of the image completely crisp.
  • Total Blur Option: Applies a full Gaussian filter to the entire image container with a single click.
  • Adjustable Intensity: Fine-tune the blur radius from 2px up to 50px depending on the level of obfuscation required.
  • Real-time Rendering: Watch details fade instantly under your brush cursor using hardware-accelerated canvas filters.
  • Lossless Privacy Guarantee: Deletes the original pixels in censored areas during download, ensuring the details are unrecoverable.

Common Use Cases

  • Document Sharing: Masking names, physical addresses, bank account numbers, or signatures on invoices and bank statements.
  • Social Media Posting: Blurring faces of children or bystanders in the background of public photos before uploading.
  • Car Listings: Obscuring license plate numbers from vehicle photographs before posting them on online classifieds.
  • Technical Support: Hiding API keys, personal email addresses, or passwords in screenshots sent to support teams.

Privacy & Data Note

All image masking is executed entirely on the client side. Your uploads and retouched files are never stored, logged, or sent to any server.
