What is Image Watermark Adder

The Image Watermark Adder is a browser-based utility that lets you stamp custom copyright labels or brand logos onto your photos. By drawing text or a secondary image overlay directly onto a HTML5 canvas, the tool embeds your custom watermark into the image data.

This tool functions entirely client-side, meaning your high-resolution original images are processed in your web browser's local sandbox memory. This setup avoids server-side uploads, keeping your designs, sensitive templates, and personal photography confidential.

How to Use Image Watermark Adder

  1. Upload your base image: Click the primary dotted drag-and-drop box to select an image from your device, or drag a file directly into the drop zone.
  2. Choose watermark type: Select either the Text tab to input a custom string, or the Logo tab to upload a graphical watermark (such as a PNG logo file).
  3. Customize your watermark content: Type your copyright statement or company name in the input box, or click the logo upload button to load your brand mark.
  4. Style the overlay: Adjust the Opacity, Size, and Rotation sliders. You can select a font family (Inter, Arial, Serif, Monospace) and choose a fill color using the color picker for text watermarks.
  5. Position the watermark: Click and drag the watermark directly over your image on the preview canvas to place it exactly where you want it.
  6. Save your watermarked image: Click the Download Watermarked Image button to render and download the watermarked file to your computer.

Key Features

  • Flexible Text & Image Stamping: Toggle easily between text overlay and logo image upload options.
  • Drag-to-Position Control: Drag the watermark around the canvas to place it precisely relative to your image elements.
  • Interactive Adjustments: Calibrate watermark opacity, dimensions, and rotation angles in real-time.
  • Format-Agnostic Processing: Works with common web file extensions including JPG, PNG, and WebP.
  • Secure Sandbox Rendering: Performs all image compositing within your browser's private memory space.

Common Use Cases

  • Photographers: Placing copyright signatures or brand logos on high-resolution preview shots before sending them to clients.
  • Real Estate Agents: Overlaying agency logos or contact details onto property photos before listing them online.
  • E-commerce Stores: Marking product photos with their store name to deter competitors from hotlinking or copying images.
  • Graphic Designers: Stamping draft concepts with "SAMPLE" or "CONFIDENTIAL" to protect active work-in-progress drafts.

Privacy & Data Note

All watermarking logic executes directly in your browser. No files, logos, or text assets are uploaded to our servers, assuring complete privacy.
