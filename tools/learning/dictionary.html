What is the Dictionary?

The Dictionary is a client-side word definition utility developed to search and display word meanings instantly. By typing an English word, users can view parts of speech, phonetic transcriptions, definitions, and usage examples from a locally indexed word database.

This dictionary stores and processes your search queries locally in your browser memory. Since no network requests, remote API calls, or telemetry tasks are executed during search events, your lookup history and queries remain completely secure and private.

How to Use the Dictionary

  1. Enter Your Query: Type the word you want to search into the search input box (e.g., agile or resilient).
  2. Perform Search: Click the "Search" button or press Enter to load the definition.
  3. View Word Details: Review the results card showing parts of speech, phonetic notations, core meanings, and example sentences.
  4. Browse Recent Searches: Click on any chip in the "Recent Searches" history list to reload that word's definition instantly.

Key Features

  • Instant Meaning Lookups: Renders definitions, grammatical functions, and phonetic notation immediately.
  • Contextual Usage Examples: Displays sample sentences to help you understand how the word is used in context.
  • Automatic Search History: Saves recently searched words locally so you can click and review them during your study session.
  • Minimalist Typography: Designed with clean fonts and distraction-free layouts to keep you focused on reading.
  • 100% Secure & Private: Operates entirely in browser cache without querying external databases.

Common Use Cases

  • Reading Comprehension: Look up unfamiliar vocabulary words quickly while reading articles or documents.
  • Writing and Copywriting: Verify word meanings, grammatical types, and spelling structures before adding them to your documents.
  • Pronunciation Practice: Use the phonetic symbols to guide correct speech and vocabulary development.
  • Offline Language Learning: Study core English vocabulary words without needing an active internet connection.
