What is the Quiz Generator?

The Quiz Generator is a client-side study utility designed to convert study notes and articles into interactive multiple-choice questions. By parsing sentence structures and identifying key terms locally, the generator produces review sheets to help test your reading comprehension.

This quiz maker runs all text segmentations, keyword extractions, and distractor calculations locally in your browser memory. Your lecture notes, article drafts, and research paragraphs are never uploaded, ensuring total data privacy.

How to Use the Quiz Generator

  1. Input Study Text: Paste your article, textbook chapter, or review notes into the "Input Text" textarea.
  2. Generate the Quiz: Click the "Generate Quiz" button to launch the client-side parsing engine.
  3. Review the Questions: Answer the generated multiple-choice questions by clicking the radio buttons next to the options.
  4. Submit Answers: Review your score and correctness indicators to identify areas that need restudy.

Key Features

  • Automated Question Generation: Automatically extracts key nouns and concepts to build sentence-completion questions.
  • Dynamic Distractors: Generates plausible multiple-choice options from other parts of the text to test real recall.
  • Instant Feedback Indicators: Colors options immediately after submission to highlight correct and incorrect choices.
  • Simple User Workspace: Features a side-by-side layout with text inputs on the left and quiz results on the right.
  • 100% Client-Side Engine: Operates entirely in browser cache without requiring external API lookups.

Common Use Cases

  • Active Recall Study Sessions: Paste textbook summaries to self-test and review key facts instead of reading passively.
  • Pop Quiz Drafting: Help teachers and educators generate quick conceptual quizzes for students.
  • Professional Certification Prep: Review technical definitions, process rules, and terms for IT or finance exams.
  • Reading Comprehension Checks: Verify vocabulary and key details from newly read articles.
