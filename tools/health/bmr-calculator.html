What is Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)?

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) is the minimum amount of energy (expressed in calories) required to sustain your body's vital life-support functions while at complete rest. These functions include breathing, circulating blood, regulating body temperature, and synthesizing cells.

This BMR Calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation—currently recognized as the clinical standard for estimating resting daily calorie needs. All calculations are executed locally in your browser memory, ensuring your age, weight, and biological details remain completely private.
