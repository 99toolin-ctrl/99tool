What is Kinetic Energy?

Kinetic energy ($KE$) is the energy that an object possesses because of its motion. It is defined as the work needed to accelerate a body of a given mass from rest to its stated velocity. The resulting formula is $KE = \frac{1}{2} m v^2$. Our online kinetic energy calculator provides an automated way to solve for this energy in Joules ($J$). This is an essential mechanical auditor tool for physics students and engineering professionals.

Impact of Velocity on Energy

Because velocity is squared in the formula, doubling the speed of an object quadruples its kinetic energy. This principle is vital for safety analysis in automotive and ballistics diagnostics.
