Measured Force (Newtons)

Based on Newton's Second Law of Motion: The force on an object is equal to its mass multiplied by its acceleration.

What is the Force Formula?

Newton's Second Law of Motion defines force as the product of mass and acceleration ($F = m \times a$). One Newton ($N$) is the amount of force required to accelerate 1 kilogram of mass at a rate of 1 meter per second squared.
