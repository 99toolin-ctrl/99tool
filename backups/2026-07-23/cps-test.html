What is a CPS Test?

CPS stands for Clicks Per Second. It is a measurement used to determine how fast you can click your mouse button or touch your screen. Our online click speed test is a refined diagnostic tool used by gamers to audit their performance. Whether you use "Jitter Clicking," "Butterfly Clicking," or "Drag Clicking," our tool provides a neutral, high-fidelity environment to measure your results.

How to Improve Your CPS

Most casual users click at around 4-6 CPS. Competitive gamers often reach 10-14 CPS. By using our CPS trainer, you can practice daily to improve your finger stamina. Consistent use of a clicking speed checker helps you identify the exact moment your finger fatigue sets in, allowing you to train specifically for endurance tests like the 10-second or 60-second modes.

Interpreting Your Results

Your clicks per second score is calculated by dividing your total clicks by the test duration. A high score indicates excellent muscle coordination. If you find your scores are inconsistent, check your hardware. A high-quality gaming mouse can significantly improve your results over a standard office peripheral. Use our mouse benchmarking tool to verify your equipment's capabilities.
