How to Configure Your Command Center (Step-by-Step)

Building your dream dashboard should be a high-speed, 10-second task. Here is how to use our **Dashboard**:

  • Step 1: Spawn Your Tools — Use the button bar to add clocks, task lists, or note pads. Our system instantly creates a new module for you to interact with.
  • Step 2: Input and Organize — Type your notes or add your "Most Urgent Actions" to the task lists. The changes are recorded in real-time as you type.
  • Step 3: Refine the Layout — Arrange your widgets to match your visual preference. A clean layout helps reduce the "Cognitive Overhead" of finding information.
  • Step 4: Commit Your Workspace — Click "Save Layout" to ensure your configuration is permanently stored in your browser. Your private setup will be ready every time you return to 99tool.in.
