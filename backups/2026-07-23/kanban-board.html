How to Conquer Your Project (Step-by-Step)

Managing your workflow should be a high-speed, frictionless experience. Here is how to use our kanban board:

  • Step 1: Load Your Backlog — Click the "+ Add Task" button in the "To Do" column. Enter your project items one by one. Our system instantly captures them in your local browser storage.
  • Step 2: Commit to Action — Ready to start? Click and hold a task card, then drag it into the "In Progress" column. This visual shift helps you focus on the current mission.
  • Step 3: Celebrate Completion — Once a task is finished, drag it into the "Done" column. Watch the task counts update in real-time, providing an instant dopamine hit for your progress.
  • Step 4: Secure Your Timeline — Use individual delete buttons to clear finished tasks or keep them on the board as a "Victory Log" of your project's history, knowing they survive browser restarts.
