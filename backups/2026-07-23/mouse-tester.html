Why Test Your Mouse Online?

A failing mouse can be the most frustrating hardware issue for both office productivity and competitive gaming. Issues like "Double-Clicking" (when a single physical click registers twice) or "Scroll Jitter" (when scrolling down causes the page to jump up) can go undetected without a precision mouse tester. Our online mouse checker is designed to catch these glitches instantly by visualizing and counting every hardware interrupt.

Testing Left, Right, and Middle Buttons

Unlike a standard "click test," a professional mouse button tester identifies the difference between standard and peripheral inputs. Our tool captures mousedown and mouseup events independently, which allows you to detect if a switch is "sticking." If you're a gamer, you can use our mouse click checker to verify the polling response of your high-performance Omron or Kailh switches.

Diagnosing Scroll Wheel Failure

Dust is the enemy of the mouse encoder. If your online scroll tester shows negative values when you are scrolling forward, your encoder likely needs cleaning. Our tool maps the vertical delta of your scroll wheel to a visual counter, making it easy to see if your mouse scroll wheel check passes the consistency test required for smooth document navigation.

Polling Rate and Sensor Precision

As you move your cursor over our mouse sensor tester, you will see real-time coordinate updates. While browsers add some latency, seeing smooth, incrementing numbers verifies that your sensor doesn't have "skipping" or "spin-out" issues.
