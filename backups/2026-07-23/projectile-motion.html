What is Projectile Motion?

Projectile motion occurs when an object is thrown or projected into the air and moves along a curved path under the influence of gravity ($g \approx 9.81\text{m/s}^2$). Our online trajectory calculator uses kinematics equations to predict its behavior. It accounts for both horizontal and vertical components of velocity to provide a high-fidelity estimation of its journey. This is a critical motion auditor tool for physics students and ballistic researchers.

Key Physics Parameters

The **Range** is the horizontal distance traveled, the **Maximum Height** is the peak vertical point, and the **Time of Flight** is the total duration the object remains in the air. By using our parabolic motion auditor, you can visualize how launch angles affect distance. For example, a 45-degree angle typically provides the maximum range in a vacuum. A consistent motion diagnostic tool is essential for optimizing launch parameters in engineering and sports science.
