What is a Reaction Time Test?

A reaction time test measures how quickly you can respond to a sensory stimulus. In this case, we use a visual color shift. When the screen turns from red to green, you must click as fast as you can. Our online reaction tester provides a high-fidelity environment that minimizes browser latency to give you a true millisecond (ms) result.

How to Improve Reaction Speed

Human reaction averages around 200ms to 250ms. Factors like fatigue, age, and distractions can slow this significantly. Competitive gamers often hover around 150ms to 180ms. By using our reflex diagnostic tool frequently, you can train your brain to recognize patterns and react faster. A consistent mental focus checker is a standard part of any professional gaming or athletic training regimen.
