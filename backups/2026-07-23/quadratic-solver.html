How to Solve Quadratic Equations

A quadratic equation is a second-order polynomial equation of the form $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$. Our online quadratic solver uses the quadratic formula to find the values of $x$ (roots) that satisfy the equation. By calculating the **discriminant ($Δ = b^2 - 4ac$)**, we can determine if the roots are real, complex, or a single repeated value. This is a vital algebra auditor tool for high school and university students.

Understanding Root Nature

If $Δ > 0$, the equation has two distinct real roots. If $Δ = 0$, there is exactly one real root (a double root). If $Δ < 0$, the roots are complex conjugates. Using our roots diagnostic auditor, you can instantly see this classification alongside numerical results. A consistent polynomial math tool is essential for curve fitting, physics modeling, and structural engineering diagnostics.
