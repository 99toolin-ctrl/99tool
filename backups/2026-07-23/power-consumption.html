How to Calculate Power Consumption

Energy consumption ($\text{kWh}$) is calculated by multiplying the wattage of a device by the number of hours it is used, divided by 1,000. The formula is: $\text{kWh} = (\text{Watts} \times \text{Hours}) / 1000$. Our online power usage calculator automates this process to provide a clear picture of your energy footprint. This electricity auditor tool is essential for managing household utility bills and optimizing energy efficiency.

Estimating Electricity Costs

Once you have the total kWh, multiply it by your local utility rate (e.g., ₹8 per unit for many Indian users) to find the total cost. By using our home appliance energy auditor, you can identify high-consumption devices and plan your usage patterns to save money. A consistent energy consumption scorecard is the first step toward a more sustainable and cost-effective home.
