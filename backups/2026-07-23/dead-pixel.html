What are Dead and Stuck Pixels?

Your screen is made of millions of tiny "sub-pixels" (Red, Green, Blue). A dead pixel is one that has lost power entirely and appears as a permanent black dot. A stuck pixel is one where one or more sub-pixels are permanently "on," appearing as a bright red, green, or blue dot. Our online dead pixel tester helps you identify these defects by cycling your display through contrasting colors in full-screen mode.

How to Test for Display Defects

The most reliable way to find a pixel defect is to use a high-fidelity solid background. On a white background, you can easily spot dead (black) pixels. On a black background, you'll see stuck (bright) pixels. Our online screen checker automates this process.
