What is Ohm's Law?

Ohm's Law is a fundamental principle in electrical engineering that states the relationship between voltage ($V$), current ($I$), and resistance ($R$). The formula is expressed as $V = I \times R$. Our online Ohm's law calculator allows you to solve for any of these three components by providing the other two. This is an essential electrical auditor tool for students, hobbyists, and professional engineers.

Understanding the Components

Voltage is the electrical pressure, current is the flow of electrons, and resistance is the opposition to that flow. By using our circuit analysis tool, you can quickly determine if an electronic component will safely handle a specific load. A standard electrical diagnostic auditor is key to preventing circuit failures and optimizing power distribution.
