What is a Numerical Derivative?

In calculus, the derivative and rate of change at a point can be estimated without complex symbolic rules by using **Numerical Differentiation**. Our online derivative solver uses the central difference method: $f'(x) \approx (f(x+h) - f(x-h)) / 2h$. This provides high-accuracy results for students and engineers who need the gradient of a curve at a specific point instantly. A consistent calculus diagnostic auditor is key to analyzing function behavior in physics and economics.
