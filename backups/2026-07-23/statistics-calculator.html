What are Descriptive Statistics?

Descriptive statistics provide a summary of a numeric dataset, helping to describe the center and spread of the data. Our online statistics solver calculates the **Mean** (average), **Median** (middle value), and **Mode** (most frequent value) instantly. It also provides measures of dispersion like **Variance and Standard Deviation**, which are critical for scientific research and financial diagnostics. A consistent dataset diagnostic auditor is essential for optimizing machine learning and data science projects.

Understanding Variance & Std Deviation

Variance ($σ²$) measures the average of the squared differences from the mean, while Standard Deviation ($σ$) is the square root of that variance, representing the average distance from the mean.
