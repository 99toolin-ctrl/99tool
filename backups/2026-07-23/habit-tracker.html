How to Build Your Streak (Step-by-Step)

Starting a new routine should be a high-speed, 5-second task. Here is how to use our habit tracker:

  • Step 1: Define the Mission — Click "+ Add New Habit" and name your goal. Be specific (e.g., "30-min Reading" rather than just "Read").
  • Step 2: Commit Daily — Visit the tool every day and click the corresponding date on the calendar. Our system updates your "Streak Fire" instantly.
  • Step 3: Analyze Consistency — Use the colored grid to spot patterns. Identifying missed days helps you understand your "Friction Points" and adjust your environment.
  • Step 4: Backup Your Wins — Use the "Backup Data" button regularly. This creates a local JSON file that ensures you never lose years of progress due to a browser clear.
