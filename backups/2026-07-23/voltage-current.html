What is the P=VI Formula?

The **Power Formula ($P = V \times I$)** defines the relationship between electrical power (Watts), potential difference (Voltage), and flow rate (Current). It is the standard calculation used to determine the electrical load of a device or the capacity needed for a circuit breaker. Our online voltage and current solver provides an automated way to audit these metrics for any electrical project.
