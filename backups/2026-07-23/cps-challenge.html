What is the CPS Challenge?

The **CPS Challenge** is a competitive clicking game designed to test the limits of human finger speed. While standard tests simply give you a number, the challenge ranks your performance based on established gaming community benchmarks. Our online CPS game provides a professional, high-fidelity environment to earn your rank and prove your clicking skills.

Understanding the Rank System

Ranking is based on your sustained Clicks Per Second (CPS). A score of 0-4 is average for casual use, while reaching "Godlike" (14+ CPS) usually requires advanced techniques like Jitter or Butterfly clicking. By playing our click challenge tool regularly, you can track your progress as you move from "Turtle" to "Lightning" status.
