What is Pressure?

In physics, **Pressure ($P$)** is defined as the force applied perpendicular to the surface of an object per unit area over which that force is distributed ($P = F / A$). The SI unit for pressure is the Pascal ($\text{Pa}$), which is equivalent to one Newton per square meter ($\text{N/m}^2$).
