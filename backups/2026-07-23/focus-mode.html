The Logic of Auditory Immersion

Focus isn't just about silence; it's about "Auditory Isolation." Most basic distraction blockers simply act as a timer, leaving you susceptible to sudden noises in your physical environment. Our professional focus mode takes a human-centric approach: we focus on **Procedural Soundscapes** and **Binaural Consistency**.

We utilize the Web Audio API to generate real-time, non-repeating sounds like steady rain or ocean waves.
