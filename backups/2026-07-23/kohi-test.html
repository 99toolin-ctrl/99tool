What is the Kohi Click Test?

Originally popularized on the Kohi Minecraft server, the **Kohi Click Test** has become a global standard for measuring clicking speed. While many tests offer various time modes, the Kohi standard is strictly 10 seconds. This duration is long enough to test finger endurance but short enough for focused bursts. Our online Kohi tester provides the high-fidelity feedback required for precise skill audits.

Why Gamer's Use Kohi Standards

In Minecraft PvP, CPS (Clicks Per Second) directly influences your hit detection and knockback consistency. Pro players often aim for 12+ CPS using refined techniques. Our CPS checker allows you to compare your results against community benchmarks. If your Kohi test score is lower than expected, your hardware or debounce settings might be the limiting factor.
