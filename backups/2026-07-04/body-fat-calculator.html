What is the Navy Body Fat Method?

The U.S. Navy Method is a widely respected formula used to estimate body fat percentage without expensive clinical equipment. It relies on specific circumference measurements of the neck, waist, and (for women) hips. Our online body fat calculator provides a high-fidelity estimation of your body composition, which is often more useful than weight alone for tracking fitness progress.

Why Body Fat Percentage Matters

Your weight on a scale doesn't distinguish between muscle, bone, and fat. By using our body composition auditor, you can ensure that your lifestyle changes are resulting in healthy fat loss rather than just muscle depletion. Athletes typically aim for 6-13% for men and 14-20% for women, while healthy fitness ranges fall between 14-24% for men and 21-31% for women.
