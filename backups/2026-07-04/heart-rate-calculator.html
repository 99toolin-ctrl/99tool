What are Heart Rate Zones?

Heart rate zones are percentages of your maximum heart rate (Max HR) that correlate with different levels of exercise intensity. Training in specific zones helps you target different physical goals, such as fat oxidation, aerobic endurance, or anaerobic capacity. Our online heart rate calculator uses the Fox formula (220 - age) to provide an essential baseline for your fitness training.

Understanding Your Training Intensity

The "Fat Burn" zone (50-70% of Max HR) is ideal for long-duration activities, while the "Aerobic" zone (70-85%) significantly improves cardiovascular health. By using our private heart rate auditor, you can ensure you are training at the correct intensity. This secure heart rate diagnostic helps you avoid overtraining and optimize every fitness session for maximum results.
