How to Backup Your Setup (Step-by-Step)

Migrating your configurations should be a high-speed, 5-second task. Here is how to use our local backup tool:

  • Step 1: Audit Your Data — View the dashboard to see how many data points are currently stored in your browser's local 99tool sanctuary.
  • Step 2: Generate the Export — Click "Create Backup File." Our system locally compiles your data into a secure JSON file and triggers a direct download.
  • Step 3: Transfer Securely — Move the file to your new device or storage medium. Since it's a standard text file, you can even inspect the content yourself.
