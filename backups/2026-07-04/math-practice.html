The Cognitive Benefits of Mental Math

Regular practice with numbers increases your "Working Memory" capacity—the ability to hold and manipulate information in your head. Our math exercises tool triggers high-frequency neural firing by presenting problems at the edge of your current ability level. This is what psychologists call "Deliberate Practice."

  • Beginner: Basic addition and subtraction (1-2 digits).
  • Intermediate: Multi-operation problems and simple multiplication.
  • Advanced: Large digit multiplication, division, and mixed logic.
