Transform Reading into Active Learning

Passive reading is often an inefficient way to retain information. Our quiz generator helps you transition to "Active Recall" by turning your study materials into interactive tests. Whether you are a student preparing for exams or a teacher creating quick classroom check-ins, our free quiz maker provides a fast, automated way to challenge your knowledge.

How the Rule-Based Generator Works

Our tool uses a deterministic linguistic algorithm to identify key sentences and important terms within your text. It selectively hides critical keywords to create "fill-in-the-blank" questions and generates plausible distractors from the surrounding context. Because this happens entirely in your browser using JavaScript, your source material is never uploaded to the cloud.

  • Analyze: We scan the text for high-value nouns and proper names.
  • Generate: We create questions based on factual statements in the text.
  • Test: We provide instant feedback on your answers to reinforce memory.
