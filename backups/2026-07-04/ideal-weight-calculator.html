What is Ideal Body Weight (IBW)?

Ideal Body Weight is a goal weight set by medical and fitness professionals based on a person’s height and biological sex. It is not necessarily the weight at which a person "looks" best, but rather the weight associated with the lowest health risks. Our online ideal weight calculator uses the Devine formula, which has been the medical standard since 1974.

Interpreting Your Weight Target

Every individual’s body is different. Factors like muscle mass, frame size, and athletic activity influence what weight is "right" for you. By using our private weight auditor, you can find a healthy middle-ground. We provide both a specific ideal weight score and a healthy range estimation (based on a BMI of 18.5 to 25).
