The Science of Effortless Remembering

Forget the frustration of traditional rote memorization. Our flashcard app leverages the powerful principle of Spaced Repetition (SRM) to help you learn faster and retain information longer. Unlike physical cards that get lost or cluttered, our digital workspace organizes your learning into a structured system that adapts to your memory.

How Spaced Repetition Works

Your brain is wired to forget information it doesn't use. The Leitner System, which our tool uses, fights this "forgetting curve" by showing you difficult cards more frequently and easy cards less often. This forces your brain to recall information right at the point where it was about to be forgotten, which significantly strengthens the neural pathways associated with that knowledge.

  • Easy: Card returns in a few days.
  • Medium: Card returns tomorrow.
  • Hard: Card returns in the next session.
