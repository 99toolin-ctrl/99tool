What is BMI?

BMI stands for **Body Mass Index**. It is a simple calculation that results in a single number used to determine if a person is at a healthy weight for their height. Our online BMI calculator provides a professional-grade baseline to help you understand your body composition category.

Interpreting Your BMI Results

A BMI of 18.5 to 24.9 is generally considered "Normal" or healthy. Scores below or above this range may indicate potential health risks. However, use this private health auditor with context; BMI does not account for muscle mass or bone density. A professional athlete and a sedentary individual might have the same BMI but different body compositions.
