Why Manual Curation Beats Pre-Made Lists

Most vocabulary apps online overwhelm you with lists of 10,000+ words you might never use. Research in cognitive linguistics shows that you are 4x more likely to remember a word if you find it in the wild (a book, a video, or conversation) and manually record its meaning. Our word learning tool facilitates this by providing a clean, focused interface to store your discoveries.

  • Discovery: Come across a new word while reading.
  • Recording: Save it privately with a meaningful context example.
  • Review: Search and review your collection regularly to solidify the memory.

Data Sovereignty for Learners

Your learning progress is personal. Many vocabulary apps track your struggle points and word associations to sell targeted ads or study subscriptions. At 99tool.in, we believe in 100% privacy.
