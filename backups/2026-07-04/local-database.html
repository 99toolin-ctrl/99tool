How to Manage Your Data (Step-by-Step)

Storing and viewing your records should be a high-speed, zero-friction task. Here is how to use our mini database:

  • Step 1: Initialize Your Table — View your active table dashboard. The tool uses a default schema of ID, Name, and Data to get you started immediately.
  • Step 2: Add Your Records — Click "+ Add Record." Enter your information into the modal form. Our system validates the entry and saves it locally.
  • Step 3: Organize and Scale — Continue adding records to build your collection. The tool keeps them persisted in your browser even if you close the tab.
  • Step 4: Export Your Data — Click the "Export" buttons to grab your collection as a CSV or JSON file, allowing you to use your data in other professional applications.
