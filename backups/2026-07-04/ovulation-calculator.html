What is the Fertile Window?

Your **fertile window** is the time in your menstrual cycle when pregnancy is possible. It consists of the day of ovulation and the five days preceding it. Our online ovulation calculator helps you estimate these key days by analyzing your cycle length and last period date. This private reproductive auditor provides a high-fidelity baseline for understanding your body's rhythm.

How Ovulation Timing Works

Ovulation typically occurs about 14 days before your next period begins. For a standard 28-day cycle, this is often on day 14. However, cycle lengths vary. By using our secure cycle checker, you can adjust for your personal average.
