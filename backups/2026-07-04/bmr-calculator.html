What is Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)?

BMR is the amount of energy (in calories) your body needs for its most basic functions, such as breathing, heart rate, and cell production. It is the number of calories you would burn if you stayed in bed all day. Our online BMR calculator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation—currently the most accurate clinical standard for predicting metabolic requirements.

How BMR Influences Weight Management

Your BMR makes up about 60-75% of your total daily energy expenditure. By understanding your resting metabolic rate, you can create more effective nutrition plans. If you are aiming for weight loss, knowing your metabolism baseline helps you set a safe calorie deficit without slowing down your body's essential processes.
