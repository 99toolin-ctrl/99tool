Why Daily Water Intake Matters

Water is essential for every biological process in your body, from temperature regulation to joint lubrication and waste removal. Dehydration by even 2% can significantly impair your cognitive function and physical performance. Our online water intake calculator provides a customized hydration baseline to keep you functioning at your best.

Factors Influencing Hydration

Biological weight is the primary factor, as larger bodies require more water to sustain cellular processes. However, activity and environment play massive roles. By using our hydration audit tool, you can account for daily physical exertion. If you live in a hot climate or engage in intense exercise, your daily water needs scorecard will reflect those requirements.
