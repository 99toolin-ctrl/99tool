How to Build Your Private Snippet Library (Step-by-Step)

Organizing your digital knowledge should be a frictionless, 30-second task. Here is how to use our code snippet manager:

  • Step 1: Securely Load Your Snippet — Click "+ Add New Snippet." Enter a descriptive title and paste your code into the workspace. Your data is instantly committed to local storage.
  • Step 2: Add Structural Tags — Label your snippet (e.g., "python", "auth", "ready-to-use"). This creates the metadata needed for lightning-fast filtering later.
  • Step 3: Find Anything Instantly — Use the global search bar. As you type, our engine filters your vault in real-time, highlighting the titles and tags that match.
  • Step 4: One-Click Deploy — Click "Copy" on any snippet card. The code is instantly copied to your clipboard, ready to be dropped into your project without the clutter of extra formatting.
