Understanding WPM and Accuracy Metrics

Speed is irrelevant without precision. Our tool calculates Words Per Minute (WPM) using the standard formula: (Total Characters / 5) / Time. However, we also place a heavy emphasis on Accuracy. Professional touch-typists aim for consistent speeds of 60-80 WPM with at least 98% accuracy. Training with our typing practice tool helps you build the muscle memory required to hit these benchmarks.
